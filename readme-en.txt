■ Greetings ■

Thank you for downloading "Rider" Ver. 2, a motorcycle courier racing game created by our company.

The prototype for "Rider" was actually a program created internally for a certain project. Unfortunately, that project was canceled, leaving behind only the prototype. Later, taking advantage of the renewal of our website, we thought we would like everyone to use this prototype, so we tweaked it slightly and completed it as freeware. Afterwards, with the cooperation of a real motorcycle courier company, "Bike Kyubin," we exhibited the game at Tokyo Game Show 2005. Taking this opportunity, we upgraded the game and created the current Ver. 2.

However, because it was originally a prototype and was upgraded in a short period of less than six months after the release of Ver. 1, the level of completion is not fully refined, and there are still some unsightly issues remaining in certain situations. We ask for your understanding. Regarding the occurrence of bugs or errors, we have set up a bulletin board, so we would appreciate it if you could report them there. We apologize, but please note that responses may be delayed or we may not be able to reply in some cases. Additionally, please understand that we do not accept inquiries by phone.

■ Installation ■

Place the downloaded "Setup.exe" into any folder and double-click it.
If you do not change the installation destination, a folder named  will be created in the same folder as "Setup.exe", and the game will be installed there.
Once extraction is complete, "Rider.exe", a  folder, and "Sound.dll" will be created under the  folder.
Basically, preparation is complete with this.
There is one point to note:
If the attribute of the  folder is set to "Read-Only", the game will not operate properly. Please make sure to uncheck "Read-Only" in the folder properties before using.
Out of consideration for your environment, "Rider" does not use the registry.

---

■ Uninstallation ■

Deleting "Rider" Ver. 2 is easy. Since it does not use the registry, simply deleting the entire  folder where the program is stored allows you to remove it without leaving any impact on your system.
It will not appear in "Add/Remove Programs" in the Control Panel. Please delete it manually using the method above.

■ How to Start ■

Please double-click Rider.exe. A small launcher menu containing the freeware confirmation text will open.
Clicking the "Game Start" button located in the middle will start the game.
On this screen, you can view graphics performance information and use the link button to Crest's homepage.
Clicking the "Game Start" button switches the screen and moves to the delivery slip format menu.

---

■ How to Exit ■

・On the delivery slip menu screen, exit using the "Exit" button at the bottom right of the screen.

・On the bike selection menu, press the "ESC" key to return to the delivery slip menu screen.

・During gameplay, pressing the "ESC" key pauses the game and displays "Exit Yes/No".
Pressing the "Y" key on the keyboard returns to the delivery slip menu.
Pressing "N" resumes from the paused state.

■ Controls for Each Screen ■

1. Launcher Menu
Click the "Game Start" button in the middle of the screen to proceed to the delivery slip menu.
The top of the menu displays freeware confirmation text.
The bottom of the menu displays graphics information for your device.
2. Delivery Slip Menu
① Past Results
Displays the status of completed deliveries.
Clicking "OK" returns to the delivery slip menu.
② Settings
・BGM (Music)
You can toggle music on/off and specify its volume.
・Sound Effects (Engine sound, etc.)
You can toggle sound effects on/off and specify their volume.
・Reset User Data
Resets partially cleared game data to start over from the beginning.
Select "Yes" and press the "Update" button.
・Screen Mode
Window / FullScreen can be selected.
Window mode is for debugging, so operation may be unstable.
Please use FullScreen as much as possible.
③ Departure
Pressing the departure button proceeds to the next bike selection screen.
④ Exit
Exits "Rider".
3. Bike Selection
Available bikes are displayed on this screen. Bikes with closed shutters cannot be used.
Moving the mouse over a bike image makes the bike start rotating. Clicking in this state starts the game with that bike.
There are a total of 6 bikes featured in this game. Use the [→] or [←] buttons to display other bikes that do not fit on the screen.
Also, bikes labeled "Totaled" (廃車) on the shutter have exceeded their damage limit and become unusable.
If you do not exceed the damage limit during a game, the bike will automatically be fully repaired by the next delivery.
Only Bike No. 1 will always have a brand-new machine prepared for the next delivery, even if it suffers damage resulting in a total loss.
_As the scenario progresses, more bikes will become available.
4. Game Screen
Controls
Clicking to choose a bike starts data loading.
After waiting a moment, an animation showing a walk through a virtual city and mounting the bike will be displayed. Once riding begins, the mouse serves as the handlebars, accelerator, and brake.
After that, all controls are done with the mouse except for the "ESC" key used to exit midway.
Please note that arrow keys and number keys are not supported.
Mouse control is determined by its position within the green frame in the center of the screen.
Even if the mouse goes outside the green frame, it will not register beyond that limit.
The trick is not to move the mouse too drastically.
The upper half represents the accelerator opening degree, and the lower half represents the braking force.
When the mouse is in the accelerator position, the pointer appears as a yellow triangle; when in the lower braking position, it appears as a blue downward triangle.
The middle mouse button allows you to look into the distance. Please note that near objects become invisible while looking far ahead.
The left and right mouse buttons allow you to look behind.

* If you turn the handlebars while holding the brakes, you can move sideways in place without moving forward.
(Use this when you get stuck between objects and cannot move.)
* Occasionally, you may get stuck between cars and be unable to move. In that case, press the "ESC" key to abort that delivery and try again.
(Unfortunately, cases like this may occur.)

■ Miscellaneous ■

* Screen Description
The gauge box at the top right contains two horizontal bar gauges: bike position and time.
The top gray bar is the bike position, and the bottom three-colored bar is time.
If the top gauge reaches the right edge before the bottom gauge reaches the right edge, it is a success.
Below that is a thick horizontal damage gauge, which decreases according to damage values.
When this gauge turns entirely gray, it indicates that the bike's durability has reached 0.
* Drivable Areas
Drivable areas are limited to roadways only. Although sidewalks may look drivable at first glance, driving on sidewalks is prohibited under the rules of this game.
Driving in the oncoming lane is not restricted by game rules, but if a police officer is present on the sidewalk of the oncoming lane, you may be ticketed.
Elevated roads, intersections, and railroad crossings have been added since Ver. 2, but you cannot drive under elevated structures, make left/right turns at intersections, or enter onto tracks at railroad crossings.
* Driving Precautions
_Speed limit signs are posted in various places. If you drive over that speed limit and police enforcement is present, you will be apprehended, forced to stop, and lose time.
_In addition, you must naturally obey traffic lights. Running a red light while a police officer is present will result in apprehension, a forced stop, and time loss.
* Bike Damage
_Bike damage is calculated based on relative speed with the collided object.
_If damage from a single collision exceeds a certain value, you will restart from the shoulder, resulting in lost time.
_Damage accumulates, and if it exceeds the bike's durability, that delivery fails.
_Furthermore, the bike will be totaled and can no longer be used.
* Game Over (Delivery Failure)
_There are two types: exceeding the bike damage limit mentioned above, and running out of time before reaching the destination.
_Also, if speed enforcement catches you exceeding the limit by more than 50 km/h, it is an instant Game Over, returning you to the menu screen.
* Delivery Success
_Reaching the destination within the specified time while keeping the bike's durability above 0 counts as a success.
_Upon success, you can proceed to the next delivery.
* End of Game
_When all deliveries are completed, staff credits will roll and the game ends.
_By selecting Settings from the delivery slip menu and performing "Reset User Data", you can start over from the beginning again.
